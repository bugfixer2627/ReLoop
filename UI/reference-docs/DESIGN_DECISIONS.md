# Design Decisions

## Current: official ReLoop brand kit

The official brand kit supersedes the earlier identity exploration below. ReLoop uses the supplied raster wordmark, infinity mark, process loop, and waveform, with Inter hosted locally. Chinese uses Microsoft YaHei / PingFang SC / Noto Sans CJK SC fallback fonts. Tokens are forest `#051C12`, lime `#B4F105`, canvas `#F4F6F5`, white `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B130F`, and border `#E9EFEF`.

Spark retains the fixed 280px sidebar, sticky search/Create/profile header, 24px cards, forms, tables, and centered login. Crypgo retains marketing navigation, two-column composition, section flow, and subtle entrance transitions. ReLoop's website mockup determines the white landing surface, dark CTAs, negative space, and process illustration.

No invented testimonial or fictional social statistic is included in marketing content. Workspace metrics and findings remain labeled mock examples. The prototype makes no root-cause verification claim. Original alternatives remain available in Design Lab and prior screenshots; they do not silently replace official tokens. See BRAND_ASSETS.md for the complete asset inventory.

The following sections record the earlier template decisions for reference; current identity choices above take precedence.

## Approved starting direction

- Spark Admin is the application reference: dark forest `#051C12`, lime `#B4F105`, light dashboard `#F4F6F5`, white surfaces, and understated green-gray borders.
- The application shell uses a fixed forest sidebar, white sticky header, compact data tables, restrained cards, and clear status colors.
- Crypgo informs only the public landing page: open marketing navigation, generous hero layout, product preview, feature sections, and simple calls to action. Cryptocurrency content and graphics are excluded.
- One Tailwind-based component system owns the UI. The references are not imported as global CSS frameworks.
- English and Simplified Chinese are shown in typography samples. System fallbacks are retained so CJK text remains readable when web fonts are unavailable.

## Alternatives retained for comparison

The design lab includes a pine/mint palette option, alternate text treatments, surface examples, navigation states, status badges, voice controls, dialog, loading, and empty states. The alternative palette is a preview only. Changing a sample does not replace the approved tokens.

## Prototype boundaries

Login, analysis, voice recording, revision chat, approval, notification, and save actions are local visual interactions. No service is contacted. Example legal and platform findings are illustrative and must not be treated as advice or actual policy determinations.
